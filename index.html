About 🐸

Anastasia Salter is a Professor of English at the University of Central Florida, where she directs the Graduate Programs and the PhD in Texts & Technology in the College of Arts & Humanities. She is President of the Electronic Literature Organization and serves as Inventio editor for Kairos: A Journal of Rhetoric, Technology, and Pedagogy.

Her work explores the future of storytelling — across electronic literature, video games, Twine and hypertext, and the messy frontier of generative AI and critical making — with a recurring focus on gender and marginalized voices in technology. She is the author of ten books on digital culture and platforms.
